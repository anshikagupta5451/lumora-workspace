Questions, answered

Is Lumora really free?

Yes. Lumora is free to download and use, with no ads.

When is the iPhone version coming?

We’re working on it. Leave your email below and we’ll tell you the day it lands on the App Store.

Why does Lumora ask for my location?

Only to fetch local weather — heat, humidity and UV — so your daily brief can adjust your routine. You can say no and still use the rest of the app.

Why does it need the camera?

To scan product barcodes and to attach photos to community posts, only when you choose to.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Email us at and we’ll delete your account and data.
